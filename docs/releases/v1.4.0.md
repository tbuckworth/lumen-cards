Open or update the app: https://tbuckworth.github.io/lumen-cards/

Lumen 1.4 helps organise a growing library:

- Deck groups. A Lumen deck file can name a `group` beside its `title`, and Decks then lists that deck under the group's heading (for example Materia Medica, then Anatomy & Physiology), with any ungrouped decks under Other decks. Importing the same deck again with a group added moves it into the group; its cards, schedules and history stay as they are.
- Study by tag. On a deck's page, choose tags under **Study by tag**: **Only these** studies the cards carrying any chosen tag, and **All except these** studies the rest. Lumen remembers the choice for that deck, and the deck's due count, Study button and Study ahead follow it. New cards still come from that deck's daily allowance, and **Begin** on Today still includes every card.
- Clearer failures. If saving, importing, starting up or preparing offline use fails, Lumen now says why and offers a retry instead of waiting silently, and a malformed import is rejected before any card is written.

Thank you to Tuula for both suggestions.

Open your existing Lumen app while online and tap **Update now** if offered, or close and reopen it. Check the bottom of Settings says **Lumen 1.4**. Your decks, images and review history stay on your device. Nothing stored is migrated or rewritten: the two new deck settings are added only when you use them, and there is no need to reinstall or clear anything.

The ZIP contains the compiled static web build. For normal use, open the app link above in Safari and Add to Home Screen. To self-host, serve the ZIP contents under `/lumen-cards/` over HTTPS (or localhost).
