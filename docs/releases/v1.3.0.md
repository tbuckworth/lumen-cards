Open or update the app: https://tbuckworth.github.io/lumen-cards/

Lumen 1.3 changes the order you meet your cards in:

- New cards now come first. A session starts with the day’s new cards and then moves on to recall, so you no longer have to get through every review before reaching something new.
- Every session is shuffled. New cards are mixed together, then reviews are mixed together, across decks on Today. Cards that sit next to each other in a deck (all the Burdock cards, then all the Calendula ones) no longer arrive in a row, so their order gives nothing away.
- Only the order has changed. Which cards are due, each deck’s daily new-card allowance, and when cards come back are decided exactly as before. New cards are still introduced in deck order from one day to the next; the shuffle happens within each session. Study ahead works the same way.

Thank you to Tuula for both suggestions.

Open your existing Lumen app while online and tap **Update now** if offered, or close and reopen it. Check the bottom of Settings says **Lumen 1.3**. Your decks, images and review history stay on your device. Nothing stored is migrated or rewritten, and there is no need to reinstall or clear anything.

Validation: 30 unit tests and 32 browser tests passed across iPhone WebKit, mobile Chromium and desktop Chromium; one existing WebKit offline-emulation test is skipped. New coverage checks that new cards come before recall, that sessions are shuffled, and that each card is graded once with its schedule otherwise untouched. These are emulated devices, not a physical-iPhone test.

The ZIP contains the compiled static web build. For normal use, open the app link above in Safari and Add to Home Screen. To self-host, serve the ZIP contents under `/lumen-cards/` over HTTPS (or localhost).
