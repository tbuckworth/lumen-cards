Lumen 1.2 makes room for the evenings when you want to keep going:

- Each deck now has its own daily new-card allowance. Studying 20 new cards in Anatomy no longer makes Botany say “Resting”. The counts on Today and Decks agree with the cards you can actually study.
- When nothing is due but unseen cards remain, a quiet **Study ahead** button appears below the usual study button. On a deck it opens that deck’s remaining new cards; on Today it opens the remaining new cards across your library. You can stop whenever you like, without changing your daily setting.
- Grading and FSRS scheduling work exactly as before. Study ahead only opens the new-card gate: paused cards stay paused, and future reviews keep their dates.

Thank you to Tuula for the careful diagnosis and the clear description of what would help.

Open your existing Lumen app while online and tap **Update now** if offered, or close and reopen it. Check the bottom of Settings says **Lumen 1.2**. Your decks, images and review history stay on your device; there is no need to reinstall or clear anything. The existing daily setting now applies separately to each deck.
